Balloon Inflating Deployment and Disassembly.

Equipment Check: Make sure all equipment, manpower safety aspects are
organized before starting the whole process. Inflate the balloon using the nozzle
provided by the Helium tank manufacturer. If the tank is under heavy
pressurization use a pressure reduction valve.

Inflation: Have two people during the inflation. One should hold the balloon from
trying to buoy and other to hold the balloon to the nozzle closely watch for leaks
of Helium at the valve stems. Using earplugs while inflating is highly
recommended. The template should be subsequently inserted to get the desired
diameter of 3ft. A third person would be ideal for this process.

Make sure to prevent sharp edges of the tank or on the ground from impinging on
the balloon surface.

Clamping and Securing Balloon: Once balloon is inflated to the desired diameter
fold the stem of the balloon. (Use width of you three middle fingers). Clamp the
folded edge. Now using masking tape wrap the folded area around the stem.

Tying Spectra to Balloon: Take the Spectra 2000 and wrap it around the stem of
the balloon. Try at best to tie around the taped area. This prevents the spectra
from cutting the balloon surface. Wind the spectra around the stem many times
before putting the fisherman’s knot. Again wrap the knotted area and the knot
with masking tape. This would further prevent the spectra from slipping in case of
a knotting failure.

Use the capture net to place the balloon if sufficient manpower is not available.
Until the Skyscout and telescope tripod assembly is ready to use.

Deployment: Deploy the balloon gently while loosely holding the tether line. If
wind gusts deploying the balloon would be chaotic and difficult. Deploying the
balloon faster to about 100 feet – 200 AGL we would observe a reduction in
chaotic movement of the balloon. Gently slow down balloon deployment when
required altitude is reached. This is to prevent sudden jolt of the tether line as you
know the tether could not take over 20lbs of force.

During the whole deployment procedures watch for cycle computer readings.

Recovery: Recovering the balloon should be done gently without jolting the tether
line. Maintain a steady speed while winding back the tether. One person should
crack the winch other guides the line by loosely holding the line. Now the balloon
is captured use the net to place the balloon if a retest is to take place.
Deflating Balloon: Use a Swiss knife or small scissors to cut the masking tape
trough the folded area of the stem. Eventually you would cut through the tether
knots. Make sure you don’t pierce the balloon wall. Now the deflated balloon
could be reused.
