# Getting help

- **How do I use a calculator? What does this result mean?** Start the guided tour (the compass button at the top of
  any page), then open the calculator's *i* buttons and its theory page. Still stuck? Ask in
  [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/RocketCalculator/RocketCalculator.github.io/discussions).
- **I think a result is wrong.** Open a
  [*Wrong result* issue](https://github.com/RocketCalculator/RocketCalculator.github.io/issues/new?template=wrong-result.yml)
  with the Share link and the source you compared against.
- **Something is broken on the page.** Open a
  [*Bug* issue](https://github.com/RocketCalculator/RocketCalculator.github.io/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
- **Idea for a feature or a new calculator.** Start in
  [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/RocketCalculator/RocketCalculator.github.io/discussions), or open a
  [*Feature* issue](https://github.com/RocketCalculator/RocketCalculator.github.io/issues/new?template=feature.yml).
- **Security problem.** Privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Rocket Calculator is maintained by volunteers. It gives engineering estimates, not flight-safety certification:
check results against your own judgement and your range's rules before you fly.
