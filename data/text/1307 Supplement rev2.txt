Introduction
       The justly famous NACA Report 13072 is the standard reference for estimating
aerodynamic interference among the several components of a rocket. However, for a
configuration with two, or more, sets of fins, 1307 is, in a practical sense, incomplete.
The purpose of this memo is to document an approach to closing these gaps.
